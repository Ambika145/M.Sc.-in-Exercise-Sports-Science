Programme Overview

Move Beyond Training. Understand Performance Scientifically.

Sports and fitness are evolving beyond experience and intuition.

Modern professionals need the ability to analyse movement, interpret performance data, design effective training programmes, and apply scientific principles to improve health and athletic outcomes.

The M.Sc. in Exercise & Sports Science prepares learners to bridge the gap between practical experience and scientific decision-making through interdisciplinary learning, research exposure, and applied practice.

Exercise Physiology

Sports Biomechanics

Strength & Conditioning

Sports Technology & AI

Work with wearables, data analytics, and AI tools to address modern challenges in sports, fitness, healthcare, and wellness.

Research & Biostatistics

Develop research design and biostatistics skills to read studies critically and make evidence-based decisions.

Training & Injury Prevention

Learn to screen risk, plan safe progressions, and apply prevention strategies across sport, fitness, and rehabilitation settings.

Eligibility:

• Academic Requirement

Applicants should have a Bachelor’s degree in medical, physiotherapy, and/or allied health sciences to apply for the M.Sc. in Exercise & Sports Science programme.

• Minimum Marks

Minimum 50% in undergraduate degree

Admission Process:

• The programme is designed for learners looking to build advanced expertise in exercise physiology, biomechanics, sports nutrition, performance analysis, and evidence-based sports practice.

• Step 1: Statement of Purpose (SOP)

Submit a Statement of Purpose as part of the application.

• Step 2: Interview

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an interview.

• Step 3: Academic Eligibility

Minimum 50% in undergraduate degree.

• Selection Criteria

Applicants who have secured less than 50% in their undergraduate degree will be required to appear for the JSAT (online proctored entrance test).
