Programme Overview

Move Beyond Training. Understand Performance Scientifically.

Sports and fitness are evolving beyond experience and intuition.

Modern professionals need the ability to analyse movement, interpret performance data, design effective training programmes, and apply scientific principles to improve health and athletic outcomes.

The M.Sc. in Exercise & Sports Science prepares learners to bridge the gap between practical experience and scientific decision-making through interdisciplinary learning, research exposure, and applied practice.

Eligibility:

• Academic Requirement

Applicants should have a Bachelor’s degree in Medical Sciences, Physiotherapy, or Allied Health Sciences to apply for the M.Sc. in Exercise & Sports Science programme.

• Ideal Backgrounds Include:

Exercise & Sports Science, Physical Education, Physiotherapy, Allied Health Sciences, and related health and wellness disciplines.

Minimum 60% in undergraduate degree
